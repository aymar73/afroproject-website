MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU)

Between the AfCFTA Secretariat and the Afro Monetary Architecture Project (AMAP)

Date: __________________, 2026

Place: Accra, Ghana (AfCFTA Headquarters)

Reference: AMAP-AFCFTA/2026/001

PREAMBLE

WHEREAS the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) aims to create a single continental market for goods and services, with free movement of business persons and investments;

WHEREAS the AfCFTA's success is contingent upon reducing transaction costs, eliminating currency conversion friction, and harmonizing payment systems across member states;

WHEREAS the Afro Monetary Architecture Project (AMAP) has developed a statistically validated, cointegration-based monetary framework that identifies three regional currency zones (West, East, Southern) with automated error correction mechanisms;

WHEREAS AMAP's framework aligns with AfCFTA's Protocols on Trade in Goods, Trade in Services, and Dispute Resolution;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1: OBJECTIVES

ObjectiveDescription
1.1Establish the Afro as the preferred settlement currency for intra-African trade under the AfCFTA.
1.2Integrate AMAP's Error Correction Mechanism (ECM) as a real-time monitoring tool for AfCFTA's macroeconomic convergence agenda.
1.3Develop a joint Afro Trade Settlement System (ATSS) that reduces transaction costs from 8% to ≤ 0.5%.
1.4Provide technical assistance to AfCFTA member states in achieving the cointegration criteria.

ARTICLE 4: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & DATA RIGHTS

4.1 The Afro ECM algorithm is open-source (public GitHub repository). AfCFTA may audit, modify, and deploy it independently.

4.2 Trade flow data provided by AfCFTA shall remain confidential and used only for ECM calibration and policy recommendations.

4.3 AMAP retains commercial rights to the ATSS software but grants AfCFTA a perpetual, royalty-free license for internal policy use.

ARTICLE 5: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

5.1 Disputes shall be resolved through mediation by the African Union's ECOSOCC.

5.2 If mediation fails, disputes shall be submitted to the African Court of Justice.

5.3 The ECM output shall be considered admissible evidence in any dispute regarding monetary policy non-compliance.

ARTICLE 6: FUNDING & RESOURCES

ClauseDetail
6.1AfCFTA shall allocate $500,000 annually to support the Afro pilot.
6.2AMAP shall match AfCFTA's contribution through seed funding and/or in-kind contributions.
6.3Additional funding may be sought jointly from Afreximbank, AfDB, and the EU.

ARTICLE 7: REVIEW & TERMINATION

7.1 This MOU shall remain in force for 5 years, renewable by mutual agreement.

7.2 Either Party may terminate with 90 days' written notice.

7.3 Upon termination, all Afro trade settlements shall revert to national currencies within a 12-month transition period.
