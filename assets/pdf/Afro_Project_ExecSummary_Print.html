Key Insight

The Enforcer: Error Correction Mechanism (ECM)

  • VECM runs quarterly. Deviation > 2σ for 2 quarters → Voting rights reduced by 10%.
  • Deviation > 2σ for 4 quarters → Automatic suspension.
  • No human override — enforced by Hyperledger smart contract.
