About the Afro Project

A PhD research initiative at McGill University, extending the cointegration + Bellman optimization framework from the CEMAC zone to the continental scale.

Founder & Lead Researcher

Aymar Makanda

Statistician-economist. Former Senior Statistical Analyst at the Bank of Canada (risk model validation, financial data under strict regulatory frameworks). PhD candidate in econometrics at McGill University.

He designed and developed the entire Afro Project prototype — from the cointegration pipeline to the MARL governance engine, including the cryptographic audit trail.

Research Affiliation

The Afro Project is a research initiative conducted at McGill University, Department of Economics, under the supervision of Prof. Francisco Ruge-Murcia (specialist in monetary policy, DSGE models, and extreme events).

It builds on the stablecoin architecture developed at Consilium-CFA, extending the cointegration + Bellman optimization framework from the CEMAC zone to the continental scale.
