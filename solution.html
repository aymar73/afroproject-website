Chapter 2

Three Zones, One Destiny

A cointegration-based monetary architecture that preserves national sovereignty while enabling continental integration.

The Afro Architecture

Each zone issues its own currency, pegged to a shared Afro-Index. All three are 100% convertible at cointegration-derived rates. The index floats against USD/EUR.

Cointegrated 3/3 pairs

Why Three Zones and Not One

The Johansen Trace Test on 20 years of data reveals that cross-zone cointegration does not exist. Only 2 out of 45 cross-regional country pairs are statistically synchronized. A single African currency would repeat the Eurozone crisis on a larger scale.

The Convergence Path

Phase 1: Digital Settlement (2027–2030)

The three Afro currencies operate as digital settlement tokens for intra-African trade. They are not yet legal tender. The goal is proof of concept: 10 million users, 1 million transactions per month.

Phase 2: Convergence (2030–2035)

Annual rebalancing of the pegs based on rolling cointegration tests. Voting rights and penalties enforced by smart contract. The three currencies become increasingly synchronized.

Phase 3: Afro-Index Launch (2035–2040)

A single floating Afro-Index is introduced. It is not a replacement for national currencies — it is a continental unit of account and reserve asset.

Phase 4: Full Integration (2040+)

The Afro-Index becomes the preferred invoicing currency for intra-African trade. Africa speaks with one monetary voice on the global stage.
