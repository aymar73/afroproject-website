The Nairobi Accord

A draft treaty for algorithmic monetary governance. It enshrines the Johansen Trace Test as the binding criterion for currency zone membership and establishes the Error Correction Mechanism as the enforcement tool.

Key provisions:

  • Cointegration as the sole gatekeeper for zone membership
  • ECM penalties enforced by smart contract, not by politics
  • Automatic suspension after 4 consecutive quarters of deviation
  • Every central bank retains its national fiscal and regulatory sovereignty
  • Dispute resolution through the African Court of Justice
